# Overview

## What is LitNexus?

LitNexus collects literature using your time window, journal list and Europe PMC queries, then supports AI translation, initial screening and human review. Mac is the primary product; Windows is a limited Preview.

## LitNexus workflow

Choose journals and queries → download records → merge into a local database → translate and classify → export for human review → import review labels.

## What does LitNexus help with?

!!! question "Why use LitNexus?"
    - Find papers for journal club.
    - Follow recent literature in a defined research area.
    - Build a custom retrieval workflow when subscriptions do not meet your needs.
    - Reduce a large set of results to a manageable human-review list.

## What does LitNexus provide?

!!! success "A customizable workflow"
    1. Retrieve records for your journals and queries within a chosen time range.
    2. Apply your screening questions through a model service.
    3. Translate titles and abstracts.
    4. Export a focused list and review it using the original text and translations.
    5. Read selected papers in your preferred tools.

!!! success "Define your own scope"
    1. Follow journals using a journal list.
    2. Follow authors or research groups with custom queries.
    3. Add topic queries to cover relevant work beyond those lists.

!!! info "Search coverage"
    Results depend on records indexed by Europe PMC, your query and your time window. LitNexus does not promise coverage of every publishing platform or full text. Check a query in Europe PMC before adding it to the app.

## What is outside its scope?

!!! note "Keep using your other research tools"
    - LitNexus does not replace Zotero or EndNote for reference management.
    - It searches the scope you specify rather than automatically discovering related topics.
    - AI screening follows your prompts and remains subject to human review.
